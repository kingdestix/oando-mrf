## Getting Started

### First Time Login

1. **Open your web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
2. **Navigate to** the MRF system URL (provided by your administrator)
3. **Enter your credentials:**
   - Email address
   - Password
4. **Click "Login"**
5. **Change your password** after first login (recommended)

### Understanding Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see a dashboard specific to your role:
- **Pending items** that need your attention
- **Statistics** relevant to your role
- **Quick actions** you can take
- **Navigation menu** to access different features

### Request Statuses Explained

- **Pending** - Waiting for approval
- **Approved** - Approved and moving to next stage
- **Rejected** - Returned to requestor for changes
- **In Progress** - Being processed
- **Delivered** - Materials delivered
- **Closed** - Request completed

## For Requestors (Workers)

### Your Dashboard

**What you'll see:**
- All requests you've created
- Status of each request
- Quick access to create new requests

### Creating a New Material Request

**Step 1: Start a New Request**
1. Click the **"New Request"** button on your dashboard
2. You'll see a form to fill out

**Step 2: Fill in Request Details**
- **Asset/Location:** Select where the materials will be used
- **Discipline:** Choose the material category (e.g., Mechanical, Electrical, Civil)
- **Reason for Request:** Describe why you need these materials (be specific!)
- **Criticality:** Select how urgent this is:
  - **Low** - Can wait
  - **Medium** - Normal priority
  - **High** - Urgent
  - **Critical** - Emergency

**Step 3: Add Materials (Line Items)**
Click **"Add Line Item"** for each material you need:

- **Material Description:** What is it? (e.g., "Gate Valve 6 inch")
- **Quantity:** How many do you need?
- **Unit:** What unit? (pcs, kg, m, etc.)
- **OEM/Model:** Manufacturer and model number (if applicable)
- **Part Number:** Part number (if applicable)

**Tip:** Add all materials you need in one request to speed up processing.

**Step 4: Submit Your Request**
1. Review all information
2. Click **"Submit Request"**
3. Your request will be assigned an MRF number (e.g., LAR-2025-001)
4. The request will automatically go to your Technical Coordinator for review

**What happens next?**
- You'll receive notifications as your request moves through approval stages
- You can track status on your dashboard
- If rejected, you'll see comments explaining why

### Confirming Material Delivery

When materials arrive:

1. **Go to your dashboard**
2. **Find the request** with status "Material Delivery"
3. **Click "Review"** button
4. **Enter received quantities** for each line item:
   - If you received less than requested, enter the actual amount
   - If you received more, enter what you actually received
5. **Check the box** "All materials received" if everything arrived
6. **Click "Confirm Delivery"**

**Important:** Only confirm delivery when you've physically received and verified the materials.

### Viewing Request Details

Click **"Review"** on any request to see:
- Complete request information
- Approval history (who approved/rejected and when)
- Current status
- Comments from approvers
- Attached documents

## For Technical Coordinators

### Your Dashboard

**What you'll see:**
- Requests waiting for your technical review
- Request details including materials needed
- Approver information

### Reviewing Requests

**Step 1: View Pending Requests**
- Your dashboard shows requests at "Technical Coordinator Review" stage
- Each request shows:
  - Requestor name
  - Location
  - Discipline
  - Date submitted
  - Criticality level

**Step 2: Review Request Details**
1. Click **"Review"** on a request
2. Review all information:
   - **Materials requested** - Are they appropriate?
   - **Quantities** - Are they reasonable?
   - **Reason for request** - Is it clear and justified?
   - **Criticality** - Does it match the urgency?

**Step 3: Make Your Decision**

**To Approve:**
1. Click **"Approve"**
2. Add comments (optional but recommended)
3. Upload your signature (optional)
4. Click **"Submit Approval"**
5. Request moves to Assistant Manager

**To Reject:**
1. Click **"Reject"**
2. **Required:** Add comments explaining why
3. Select rejection reason
4. Click **"Submit Rejection"**
5. Request returns to requestor for changes

**Important Notes:**
- You cannot see commercial details (prices, amounts) - this is by design
- Your approval confirms technical appropriateness only
- Be thorough - your review helps prevent unnecessary purchases

## For Discipline Unit

### Your Dashboard

**Title:** "Discipline Unit - Material Requests & Commercial Processing"

**What You'll See:**
- Requests in your assigned discipline
- Requests at different workflow stages
- Status indicators showing what action is needed

### Workflow Overview

You handle requests in two main stages:

**Stage 1: Discipline Review** (if at DISCIPLINE_UNIT_REVIEW)
- Review request
- Approve or reject
- Request moves to Discipline Manager

**Stage 2: Commercial Processing** (after DM approval, at COMMERCIAL_REVIEW)

This is your main responsibility - managing the commercial workflow.

### Commercial Workflow - Step by Step

#### Step 1: Send MRF to Contractor

**When:** Request shows "Step 1: Send MRF to Contractor"

**What to Do:**
1. Click **"Review"** on the request
2. Click **"Download MRF Document"** button
3. Send the PDF to your contractor/vendor
4. **Check the box:** "MRF Document Transmitted to Contractor" ✓
5. Click **"Save"**

**Why:** This creates an audit trail showing when you sent the MRF.

#### Step 2: Mark Quotation Received

**When:** Contractor sends you a quotation

**What to Do:**
1. Go to the request (still at COMMERCIAL_REVIEW stage)
2. **Check the box:** "Quotation Received from Contractor" ✓
3. Click **"Save"**
4. **Important:** The commercial details form will now appear!

#### Step 3: Fill Commercial Details

**After marking quotation received, you'll see a form:**

**Required Fields:**
- **Contractor/Vendor Name:** Who provided the quotation?
- **Quotation Reference:** Quote number or reference
- **Quotation Amount (USD):** Total amount in USD

**Optional Fields:**
- **Contract Amount (USD/EUR/NGN):** If different from quotation
- **Estimated Delivery Date:** When will materials arrive?

**File Upload:**
- **Upload Quotation PDF:** Attach the quotation document (required)

**Notes:**
- Add any additional information here

**Then:**
1. Fill all required fields
2. Upload quotation PDF
3. Click **"Submit for DODM Approval"**
4. Request moves to DODM for commercial approval

### Tracking Your Requests

**Status Indicators:**
- ⚠️ **Step 1: Send MRF to Contractor** - Action needed
- 📨 **Step 2: Mark Quotation Received** - Waiting for quotation
- ✅ **Step 3: Fill Commercial Details** - Ready to submit
- 💰 **Commercial Approved** - DODM approved
- 🚚 **In Transit** - Materials being delivered
- 📦 **Delivered** - Materials received

**Tips:**
- Keep track of requests at each stage
- Follow up with contractors if quotations are delayed
- Ensure all commercial details are accurate before submitting

## Common Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Why can't I see commercial details (prices) in the MRF PDF?**
**A:** This is by design for security. Only POD Planner, Discipline Unit, Discipline Manager, DODM, and Admin roles can see commercial details. Field workers (requestors, technical coordinators, assistant managers, area managers) cannot see prices to maintain commercial confidentiality.

**Q: Why is my request stuck at a stage?**
**A:** Check that the next approver has logged in and reviewed the request. Each stage requires approval before moving forward. If stuck, contact your system administrator.

**Q: Can I edit a request after submission?**
**A:** Only if it's rejected and returned to you. Otherwise, contact your administrator. Once approved, requests cannot be edited to maintain audit trail integrity.

**Q: How do I know who approved my request?**
**A:** Click "Review" on your request and scroll to "Approval History" section. You'll see all approvers, dates, and comments.

**Q: What if I made a mistake in my request?**
**A:** If the request hasn't been approved yet, contact your Technical Coordinator or Administrator. If already approved, you may need to create a new request or request cancellation.

### For Discipline Unit

**Q: How do I know if DM has approved my request?**
**A:** Your dashboard will show the request at "COMMERCIAL_REVIEW" stage with indicator "Step 1: Send MRF to Contractor". This means DM has approved and it's ready for commercial processing.

**Q: What if the contractor doesn't send a quotation?**
**A:** Follow up with the contractor. You can add notes to the request. If no quotation is received after reasonable time, you may need to contact your manager or select a different contractor.

**Q: Can I change commercial details after submitting to DODM?**
**A:** No, once submitted to DODM, you cannot edit. If DODM rejects, you can revise and resubmit.

### For POD Planner

**Q: How do I generate a report?**
**A:** Go to Analytics dashboard, select report period (weekly/monthly/custom), choose date range, click "Generate Report", download Word document.

**Q: Can I filter reports by location or discipline?**
**A:** Yes, use the filters on the Analytics dashboard before generating reports.

### For DODM

**Q: Why don't I see all requests?**
**A:** You only see requests that have reached COMMERCIAL_REVIEW stage (after Discipline Unit submits commercial details). Requests still in earlier stages won't appear.

**Q: Can I see requests I've already approved?**
**A:** Yes, click "Approved Requests" tab to see all requests you've approved and track their delivery status.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I can't login. What should I do?**
**A:** 
1. Check you're using the correct email and password
2. Check if Caps Lock is on
3. Contact your administrator to reset password
4. Verify your account is active

**Q: The page is loading slowly.**
**A:** 
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Clear browser cache
4. Contact IT support if problem persists

**Q: I'm getting an error message.**
**A:** 
1. Note the exact error message
2. Try the action again
3. If persists, contact your system administrator with:
   - What you were trying to do
   - The error message
   - Screenshot if possible
